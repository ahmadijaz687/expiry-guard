# Product Brief: ExpiryGuard

## 1. Executive Summary
**ExpiryGuard** is an intelligent, zero-friction kitchen inventory assistant and waste prevention system designed for modern households. Instead of functioning as another tedious grocery checklist, ExpiryGuard shifts the paradigm from passive expiration tracking to proactive culinary action through **Cook-First suggestions** and a quantified **Waste-Avoided Impact Tracker**.

---

## 2. Target User Persona
- **Persona Name**: Alex Chen, 31
- **Role**: Busy Professional & Home Cook
- **Household**: 2 adults living in an urban apartment
- **Current Behavior**: Shops once a week at farmers' markets and supermarkets; spends $140–$180 per grocery run.
- **The Core Pain**:
  - Alex buys fresh greens, dairy products, and proteins with good intentions, but work deadlines lead to takeout on Tuesday and Wednesday.
  - By Sunday, bags of wilted spinach, half-full cream cartons, and forgotten berries are discovered at the back of the fridge and tossed into the bin.
  - Feels guilty about both the wasted money (~$90/month) and the environmental impact.
  - Existing grocery apps require tedious barcode scanning or manual weight logging with complex multi-level menus.

---

## 3. The Problem & Market Gap
1. **The Forgetting Curve in Refrigeration**: Food items get pushed behind condiments and become invisible until pungent odors or mold appear.
2. **Analysis Paralysis**: When users open the fridge at 7:00 PM, they don't know what will spoil first and default to ordering food delivery, worsening the spoil cycle.
3. **No Emotional Reward for Saving Food**: Most personal finance apps only track what was spent, not what was successfully conserved or rescued from landfills.

---

## 4. The ExpiryGuard Solution
- **Visual Urgency Matrix**: Smart color-coded expiry badges (`Expiring within 48h`, `Urgent (<24h)`, `Fresh`).
- **Cook-First Engine**: Algorithmic recipe assembler that prioritizes ingredients on the brink of expiration, calculating dishes based on items Alex *must use tonight*.
- **Waste-Avoided Tracker**: Quantifies real-world impact every time an item is marked "Consumed":
  - Dollars ($) saved based on average category retail costs.
  - Weight (kg / lbs) of organic waste diverted from municipal landfills.
  - Carbon footprint prevented (kg CO₂e equivalent).
  - Consecutive waste-free weekly streak counter.
- **Smart Shelf-Life Presets**: Quick-add items with pre-calculated, research-backed refrigeration shelf lives (e.g., milk = 7 days, raw chicken = 2 days, spinach = 5 days, apples = 21 days).
- **Freeze Recommendation**: One-click action to move expiring items to the freezer, instantly updating shelf life.

---

## 5. User Journey
1. **Unpack & Quick Add**: Alex returns from shopping, taps "Quick Add" (or keyboard shortcut `N`), types or selects items from curated presets. Recommended shelf lives and optimal storage zones (Fridge crisper, shelf, or pantry) auto-populate.
2. **Daily Glances**: Alex checks the dashboard or receives daily urgency status. The banner highlights: *"3 items need your attention before Friday"*.
3. **Cook-First Action**: Alex taps "Cook First" (`C`). The engine suggests a *Quick Stir-Fry* and *Veggie Frittata* matching expiring spinach, bell peppers, and eggs.
4. **Rescued!**: Alex marks items as "Consumed". A celebratory micro-interaction triggers, and their dashboard reflects +$8.50 saved and +0.65 kg CO₂e diverted.

---

## 6. Success Metrics & KPIs
| Metric | Target | Measurement |
|---|---|---|
| Rescue Rate | > 85% of tracked items marked "Consumed" vs "Discarded" | Weekly cohort ratio |
| Average Household Savings | $65 – $110 per month | Based on synthetic valuation models |
| Time to Log 5 Items | < 30 seconds | Quick preset auto-fill benchmarking |
| Core Flow Engagement | > 70% Cook-First suggestion views | Navigation telemetry |
